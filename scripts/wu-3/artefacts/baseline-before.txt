=== Note on matrix-app health == "Progressing" ===

The matrix-app reports sync=Synced, health=Progressing at baseline. This
is benign: every Deployment has generation == observedGeneration and
availableReplicas == readyReplicas == spec.replicas. All pods 1/1 Running
for 6-7 days. Last sync operationState: phase=Succeeded at the WU-2 closure
timestamp (2026-05-13T19:18:27Z).

Why aggregate health is "Progressing" with all underlying resources healthy:
ArgoCD's per-resource health rollup shows health=null for every resource in
the status array. With no per-resource health computed, the aggregate
defaults to Progressing. This is a known aggregator quirk, not a real
unhealthy state.

Step 5's gate "matrix-app still Synced/Healthy — same as before" is read
as "same as before" here, since the baseline is Synced/Progressing.
Equivalence at post-deletion is the gate; any drift toward OutOfSync,
Degraded, or Unknown stops the verification.
